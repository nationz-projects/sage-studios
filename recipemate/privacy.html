RecipeMate privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

RecipeMate keeps your recipes, meal plans and shopping lists. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives your personal data. Everything you add stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your recipes, photos, labels, meal plans and shopping lists are saved only inside the app.
  • Your settings, such as disliked ingredients and your spice rack, are saved the same way.
  • Camera (optional): the photo button can open the camera so you can add a picture of a dish. The photo is kept in the app and never sent anywhere.

What leaves your phone

  • Discover and recipe links: open the site inside the app, the same as visiting it in your browser. The site may show its own ads and set its own cookies under its own privacy policy. When you tap Save recipe, the app copies the recipe from the page on screen and sends nothing.
  • Sharing a page to RecipeMate: your phone reads that one web page, and the site's robots.txt file first. The request carries only the usual details a browser sends and names itself as RecipeMate. Nothing about you or your recipes is sent.
  • Backups: a backup file is only created when you export one, and it goes wherever you choose to save or share it. Android may also back up your recipes, without photos, to your own Google account; you control this in your phone's settings, and the developer cannot see it.
  • Purchase: RecipeMate is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment.

Permissions

RecipeMate uses internet access to import recipes from links, and the camera only when you press the photo button. It works without either.

Children

RecipeMate is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling RecipeMate removes everything it stored. Since the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing else to delete.
